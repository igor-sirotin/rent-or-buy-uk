UK property

Rent, or buy and sell later

The methodBoth paths spend the same cash. The renter keeps the deposit and buying costs in savings; whoever pays less in a month banks the difference. The answer is what each has in hand on sale day.

Stamp dutyEngland and Northern Ireland rates, with first-time buyer relief, the 5% additional-property surcharge and the 2% non-resident surcharge. In Scotland or Wales, type your own figure.

TaxSavings interest is taxed above your allowance and outside ISAs, at the rates that apply from April 2027 (24%, 44% or 49%). A main residence is sold free of capital gains tax; otherwise 18% or 24% above £3,000.

Left outLifetime ISA bonuses, remortgage fees, moving costs between rentals, rental voids, and the plain value of not being asked to leave.
